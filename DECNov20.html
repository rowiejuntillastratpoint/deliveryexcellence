Reading the trend

What the data says

  • Red is at a new high. 15 projects (15.6%) are Red, above the previous peak of 14 on Oct 23 and the 13 on Sep 11. 22 different projects have been Red at least once across the six reports, 16 of them more than once.
  • The UD / Union Digital group is Red again. All seven of its engagements are Red (4, 0, 6, 6, 5, 7 across the reports) with a reconciliation now set for Nov 26. Red from every other project is back up to 8 after dipping to 6, because the two AWS ProServe engagements relapsed.
  • Red is now mostly a collections and funding problem. Financial concerns make up 73% of Red (46% on Sep 11); delivery-driven Red fell from 7 to 4 and is now just OKTedi (two rows), SBC ODP and MPTC.
  • Red keeps coming back. 8 of the 22 projects have left Red and then returned, including AWS ProServe AI and Data, which eased to Amber on Nov 6 and are Red again. BoxCrush Renewal, Globe Handyman and MPTC App Rebuild have been Red in every report.
  • Red plus Amber is flat, but the mix got worse. It is 25 projects (26.0%), the same count as Nov 6, down from 30.5% on Sep 11 — but 15 are now Red against 11 two weeks ago.
